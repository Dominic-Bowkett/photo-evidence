How it works

  • Tap Enable GPS once so each photo can be stamped with its coordinates.
  • Inside each group, tap Add photos to pick multiple from the gallery or take a new one with the camera.
  • Every image is re-rendered with a date/time and GPS overlay in the bottom right corner.
  • Give each photo a label (the default is the group name & number), then press Download PDF.
