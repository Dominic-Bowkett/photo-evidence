How it works

  • Tap Enable GPS once so each photo can be stamped with its coordinates.
  • Inside each group, tap Take photo to capture a shot with the device camera — the date/time recorded is the moment the photo is taken.
  • Every image is re-rendered with a date/time and GPS overlay in the bottom right corner.
  • Work is autosaved to this browser — refresh or close the tab and it's still here. Switch between multiple properties using the picker above.
